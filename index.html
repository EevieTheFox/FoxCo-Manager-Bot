FoxCo Manager Bot

Official documentation for FoxCo Manager Bot.

FoxCo Manager Bot is a private operations and management bot used exclusively within the FoxCo Armory & Marketplace (FAM) Discord server.

The Bot supports account verification, role and eligibility management, service workflows, contract coordination, in-game transaction records, notifications, moderation support, administrative logging, and related automation.

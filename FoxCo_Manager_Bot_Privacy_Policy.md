# FoxCo Manager Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 2, 2026  
**Last updated:** August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **FoxCo Manager Bot** (the **“Bot”** or **“Service”**) collects, uses, stores, protects, shares, and deletes information within the **FoxCo Armory & Marketplace** Discord server (**“FAM”**). The Bot is operated by the owner of FAM (**“FoxCo,” “we,” “us,”** or **“our”**).

This Policy applies only to FoxCo Manager Bot. Discord and Torn separately process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 1. Torn API Disclosure Summary

The following summary is intended to make the Bot’s Torn API use clear before a user provides a key.

| Topic | FoxCo Manager Bot Practice |
|---|---|
| **Data Storage** | API keys and linked-account records persist while active and needed. Operational records are generally deleted within 7 days; completed-contract records within 24 hours. After unlinking, revocation, or loss of need, remaining identifying records are deleted within 7 days, subject to limited exceptions. |
| **Data Sharing** | Within FoxCo, decrypted database access is restricted to the owner. Infrastructure providers may host encrypted data. Assigned providers receive only necessary contract details, such as a target link, bounty value, faction ID, instructions, and status. They do not receive API keys or unrelated private data. |
| **Purpose of Use** | Verification, roles, eligibility, contracts, tickets, in-game transaction records, notifications, moderation support, logging, automation, and related FAM operations. |
| **Key Storage and Sharing** | Keys are encrypted at rest and used only for authorized automation. They are not displayed or shared with providers, members, advertisers, or the public. Within FoxCo, only the owner is authorized to access decrypted keys when necessary. |
| **Key Access Level** | The lowest practical access level or custom selections needed for enabled features. Current requirements are displayed before or when the key is submitted. |

**The Bot will never ask for your Torn password.**

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the Bot may collect or process the following categories.

### A. Discord account and server information

- Discord user ID;
- username, display name, avatar reference, and account-related metadata made available through Discord;
- FAM server ID, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, thread IDs, interaction IDs, and invite-related identifiers;
- verification status, assigned roles, permissions, service-provider status, and eligibility tiers;
- command usage, button/menu selections, timestamps, success or error status, and related administrative logs; and
- server configuration selected by the owner or authorized administrators.

### B. Torn account and API information

- Torn API key;
- Torn player ID, player name, profile link, and account-linking status;
- API key information such as access level, enabled selections, validity, pause/revocation status, and request errors;
- faction ID, faction membership, company information, or other account information needed for an enabled feature;
- eligibility indicators and bucketed service classifications derived from Torn data, including service-provider or mercenary tier; and
- other Torn API data reasonably necessary for verification, role assignment, service eligibility, contract fulfillment, or enabled automation.

The exact information available depends on the permissions of the key you choose to provide and the Bot features currently enabled.

### C. Structured submissions and workflow records

- applications, order forms, rental requests, tickets, contract terms, quantities, target links, instructions, and status;
- deposits, returns, Torn-dollar or item transfers, bounty values, completion evidence, and other in-game transaction records;
- dispute, cancellation, reassignment, moderation, or support information;
- user-provided free text entered into a structured form or ticket field; and
- information needed to create, update, route, display, or delete Discord embeds and workflow messages.

The Bot does not intentionally archive ordinary Discord conversation content. It primarily processes IDs, interaction metadata, structured form submissions, and the messages or embeds that it creates or manages. Information visible in Discord may remain subject to Discord’s own systems and retention practices.

### D. Technical and security information

- timestamps, request status, errors, rate-limit information, command or API-call metadata;
- security events, failed verification attempts, suspected abuse, and access-control events; and
- infrastructure logs supplied by hosting or database providers, such as IP address, device, connection, or diagnostic information, where those providers generate such logs.

FoxCo does not use the Bot to collect payment-card information, government identification, biometric information, or Torn passwords.

## 3. How We Collect Information

We obtain information:

- directly from you when you submit a key, command, form, application, ticket, or other structured interaction;
- from Discord through the Bot API and interaction system;
- from Torn through API requests authorized by the key you provide;
- from FAM administrators when they configure roles, services, contracts, or moderation actions;
- from service providers when they update assigned contract status or submit completion evidence; and
- automatically from the Bot’s operation, security controls, and hosting infrastructure.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- link Discord and Torn accounts and confirm identity or membership;
- validate API access and maintain authorized recurring automation;
- assign roles, permissions, service claims, eligibility, and tier classifications;
- accept, route, manage, and close applications, tickets, orders, rentals, and contracts;
- provide assigned service providers with the limited operational details needed to complete a contract;
- record in-game transactions, deposits, returns, bounty values, and completion events;
- create, edit, display, and delete workflow embeds, notifications, and status messages;
- operate moderation, audit, invitation, configuration, and security functions;
- prevent fraud, duplicate submissions, manipulation, unauthorized access, and abuse;
- diagnose errors, maintain availability, and improve Bot reliability;
- respond to support, privacy, security, and dispute requests;
- enforce FAM rules and the Bot’s Terms of Service; and
- comply with applicable law and binding platform requirements.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or disclose it to data brokers.

## 5. Legal Bases Where Applicable

Where privacy law requires a legal basis, FoxCo generally processes information based on one or more of the following:

- **Consent:** you voluntarily provide a Torn API key or submit information for a requested feature;
- **Performance of a requested service or agreement:** processing is needed to provide verification, contracts, rentals, service coordination, or other Bot functionality;
- **Legitimate interests:** operating and securing FAM, preventing abuse, maintaining records for the brief retention periods described below, and improving reliability; and
- **Legal obligations:** responding to valid legal requests or complying with applicable law and platform requirements.

You may withdraw consent for future API access by revoking or deleting the key in Torn, unlinking through the Bot when available, or contacting support. Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing invalid.

## 6. Encryption, Access, and Security

FoxCo treats Torn API keys and user-identifying data as confidential.

- Torn API keys are encrypted at rest.
- User-identifying database fields are encrypted at rest.
- Within FoxCo, authorization to access decrypted database information is restricted to the service owner.
- Service providers, ordinary members, and other FAM staff do not receive database access or API keys.
- Access credentials and encryption secrets are intended to be stored separately from publicly accessible code and restricted according to operational need.
- Communications with hosting and API services will use encrypted network connections where supported.
- Security, dependency, and access controls will be maintained and updated as reasonably appropriate for the Service.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If FoxCo discovers unauthorized access that creates a legally reportable risk, FoxCo will take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, remediate, and notify affected users or authorities where required.

Infrastructure providers may technically process or store encrypted information as necessary to provide hosting, database, backup, networking, or security services. FoxCo will not intentionally provide those providers with access for unrelated purposes.

## 7. When Information Is Shared

FoxCo limits disclosure to the following circumstances.

### A. Assigned service providers

For a specific order or contract, the Bot may share only the operational information reasonably needed to perform the requested service, such as:

- the Torn profile or target link;
- the number or value of bounties;
- a faction ID for a defense contract;
- service type, instructions, timing, quantity, and status; and
- other non-sensitive contract details required for fulfillment.

The Bot does **not** share the requester’s Torn API key, encrypted database records, unrelated Discord identifiers, or unrelated Torn data with service providers.

### B. Hosting and infrastructure providers

A cloud host, database platform, logging platform, domain host, or similar provider may process encrypted data or technical logs solely to provide infrastructure. The provider selected before launch may change over time. FoxCo will choose providers based in part on cost, security, reliability, and available privacy controls.

### C. Discord and Torn

The Bot communicates with Discord and Torn as necessary to perform its functions. Those platforms process requests and related data under their own terms and privacy policies.

### D. Legal, safety, and platform compliance

FoxCo may preserve or disclose information where reasonably believed necessary to comply with law, legal process, a valid request from Discord or Torn, or to protect the security and rights of users, FoxCo, or others. Where legally permitted and practical, FoxCo will seek to limit the scope of the disclosure.

### E. With your direction

FoxCo may disclose information when you specifically request or clearly authorize the disclosure.

FoxCo does not share Bot data with advertisers or sell it to third parties.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

FoxCo uses short retention periods and deletes or anonymizes user data when it is no longer needed.

### A. Active linked accounts and API keys

Torn API keys and the minimum account-linking information needed for recurring verification or automation are retained while the account remains actively linked and the information remains necessary for enabled Bot functions.

When a key is revoked, an account is unlinked, access to FAM ends, or the information is no longer required, the key and remaining user-identifying linked-account records are scheduled for deletion within **7 days**.

### B. Operational logs and temporary records

Verification attempts, command logs, errors, temporary workflow data, invite records, moderation-support logs, and other operational records are generally deleted on a rolling basis within **7 days** of collection or closure.

### C. Active and completed contracts

Information needed for an active contract is retained while that contract remains open. Completed-contract records are generally deleted within **24 hours** after completion or closure.

### D. Discord embeds and workflow messages

The Bot may delete or destroy its embeds, temporary status messages, and workflow messages as soon as they are no longer needed. A message may be removed earlier than the database record associated with it, or vice versa, depending on the workflow.

### E. Limited exceptions

A record may be retained longer only where reasonably necessary for:

- an active dispute, fraud review, security incident, or abuse investigation;
- compliance with applicable law or a binding request from Discord or Torn;
- restoring data after a technical failure, if backups are used; or
- establishing, exercising, or defending legal rights.

When an exception ends, the information will be deleted or anonymized as reasonably practical. Backup copies, if used, may persist until the next scheduled backup expiration but will not be restored for ordinary use after a valid deletion request.

Because retention is brief, FoxCo may no longer possess a requested record by the time a user asks for it.

## 9. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You may:

- revoke or pause your Torn API key through Torn;
- unlink your Torn account using an available Bot command;
- stop using the Bot or leave FAM;
- request access to, correction of, or deletion of your stored Bot information;
- ask what categories of information are associated with you; and
- object to or restrict certain processing where applicable law provides that right.

Send requests to **foxco.manager.bot.support@gmail.com**. To protect users, FoxCo may require confirmation through the relevant Discord or Torn account before fulfilling a request. FoxCo will respond within a reasonable period and within any legally required deadline.

Some requests may be limited where FoxCo cannot verify identity, no longer has the data, must retain it temporarily under a permitted exception, or must preserve the integrity and privacy of another user’s information.

## 10. Automated Decisions

The Bot may automatically verify account data, assign roles, calculate eligibility tiers, route tickets, update contract status, or restrict access based on configured rules. These actions affect participation in FAM services, not legal rights or access to essential real-world services.

You may contact support to request review of a significant Bot decision that you believe resulted from inaccurate data or a technical error.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is not directed to children under 13. Users must satisfy Discord’s, Torn’s, and local legal age requirements. FoxCo does not knowingly seek information from a person who is not permitted to use the relevant platforms. If you believe such information has been collected, contact support so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

## 12. International Processing

The Bot, its owner, or its future hosting providers may process information in the United States or other countries. Those locations may have privacy laws different from those where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, FoxCo will use reasonable measures available to a small private community service.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

FoxCo may update this Policy when the Bot’s functions, data practices, providers, or legal obligations change. Material changes will be announced in FAM or through another reasonable notice, and the date at the top will be updated.

If a change materially expands how a stored Torn API key or private account data is used, FoxCo will provide notice and obtain additional authorization where required before applying the expanded use.
